背景：智利皮肤科市场对医美后护理产品有高功效与低刺激的需求，且需适应多样化的微气候。方法：本研究针对 RIMAN 力曼旗下的 INCELLDERM 奇迹霜进行分层调查（N=200），涵盖圣地亚哥、瓦尔帕莱索和康塞普西翁三个主要城市，评估其感官、功能及法规认知。结果：该配方展现出卓越的吸收速度（平均分 4.0，前两项比例 88%）及适合干燥高海拔气候的轻盈质地（前两项比例 77.5%）。关键成分如积雪草叶水（选择率 100%）和乙酰基六肽-8（84%）驱动了购买意图。然而，价格敏感度仍是障碍（前两项比例仅 19%），97% 的受访者将有限的分销渠道列为主要阻力。战略启示：虽然 B2B 从业者对其临床信任度高（平均分 3.88），但扩大当地临床数据发布及优化 CLP 定价策略对于将被动用户转化为推荐者至关重要。

## English overview

Background: The Chilean dermatological market demands high-efficacy, low-irritation formulations for post-procedure care amidst diverse microclimates. Methods: A stratified survey (N=200) evaluated the sensory, functional, and regulatory perceptions of RIMAN Skincare's INCELLDERM Active Cream EX across Santiago, Valparaiso, and Concepcion. Results: The formulation demonstrated exceptional absorption speed (Mean 4.0, Top-2-Box 88%) and lightweight texture suitable for dry, high-altitude climates (Top-2-Box 77.5%). Key ingredients like Centella Asiatica Leaf Water (100% selection rate) and Acetyl Hexapeptide-8 (84%) drove purchase intent. However, price sensitivity remains a barrier (Top-2-Box 19%), with limited distribution channels cited by 97% of respondents as a primary obstacle. Strateg
